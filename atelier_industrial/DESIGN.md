## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an architectural, high-end visual language tailored for global apparel manufacturing, export procurement, and bespoke textile engineering. It bridges the discipline of precision industrial manufacturing with European luxury fashion tailoring.

### Personality & Tone
- **Architectural & Rigorous:** Systematic indexing, clean lines, and measured editorial cadence communicating institutional manufacturing capacity and high-volume reliability.
- **Sartorial Luxury:** Warm champagne and metallic gold tones evoking bespoke garment craftsmanship, refined fabric finishing, and executive-level trade partnership.
- **Direct & Authoritative:** High-contrast functional clarity designed for international brand directors, procurement heads, and apparel sourcing managers.

### Design Movement
The aesthetic synthesizes **Corporate Modernism** with **Editorial Fashion Minimalism**. It balances strict structural data tables, capacity metrics, and multi-language controls with generous white space, hairline borders, and refined serif typographic accents matching the brand's metallic insignia.

## Colors

The color system leverages high-contrast deep slate foundations accented with warm metallic gold hues, reflecting the tactile quality of premium thread and high-grade loom hardware.

### Roles & Application
- **Primary (`#0F172A`):** Deep rich navy/slate used for primary typography, authoritative structural bars, dark-mode toggle components, and primary interactive states.
- **Secondary (`#C5A059`):** Refined champagne gold applied deliberately to active indicators, primary conversion CTAs, catalog index markers (`01 /`), and critical capacity callouts.
- **Tertiary (`#334155`):** Mid-tone graphite slate applied to secondary descriptions, iconography, table borders, and subtle interactive states.
- **Neutral (`#F8FAFC`):** Crisp architectural background tone that provides distinction from stark `#FFFFFF` card surfaces.

### Contrast Ratios
- `#0F172A` against `#F8FAFC` yields an AAA-compliant contrast ratio of 16.2:1 for body and data layers.
- `#C5A059` on `#0F172A` achieves a 4.8:1 contrast ratio, meeting AA requirements for large text, icons, and bordered badges. For smaller critical text elements, utilize `#9E7B35` on light surfaces or `#E2C98F` on dark panels.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy juxtaposes classical editorial serif elegance with sharp, structural sans-serif precision.

### Type Pairings
- **Display & Headings:** `Playfair Display` provides the refined tone of haute couture, luxury lookbooks, and high-end atelier tradition. It is reserved for prominent page headers, collection group showcases, and quantitative metric highlights.
- **Interface, Metrics & Body:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` provides legibility across technical specifications, fabric blend percentages, MOQs, RFQ inputs, and multi-language controls.

### Editorial Conventions
- **Overlines & Eyebrows:** Rendered in strict uppercase with `letterSpacing: 0.15em` using the dot divider format (e.g., `BSN TEXTILE · DİJİTAL KATALOG`).
- **Interactive Action Labels:** Rendered in strict uppercase with directional navigation glyphs (`↗`).
- **Index Markers:** Zero-padded tabular figures (e.g., `01 /`, `02 /`) set with prominent weights to establish serial production rhythm.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a clean 12-column architectural grid with balanced white space, reflecting the geometric order of textile cutting patterns and fabric bolt alignment.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column grid, max-width `1440px`, `3rem` (48px) lateral outer margins, and `1.5rem` (24px) gutters.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column fluid grid, `2rem` (32px) margins, and `1.25rem` (20px) gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid grid, `1.25rem` (20px) margins, and `1rem` (16px) gutters.

### Structural Cadence
- **Section Margins:** Major manufacturing capabilities and product categories are separated by `space-2xl` (64px) to `5rem` (80px) to allow imagery to breathe.
- **Component Padding:** High-density metric blocks use `space-md` (16px); collection category cards use `space-lg` (24px) internal clearance.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy avoids heavy drop shadows, relying instead on tonal architectural surfaces, structural hairline dividers, and minimal ambient glow.

### Depth Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#F8FAFC` solid foundation for page background.
- **Level 1 (Surface Containers):** Crisp pure `#FFFFFF` background for collection cards, specification drawers, and data modules, framed with a continuous `1px` border of `#E2E8F0`.
- **Level 2 (Active & Floated Elements):** Sticky internationalization header, elevated RFQ drawers, and dropdown menus utilize frosted background blurs (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) at `92%` opacity with an ultra-subtle ambient shadow: `0 10px 30px -10px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Overlays & Dialogs):** High-priority inquiry panels and image zooms leverage a solid `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.65)` backdrop with centered pure-white modal frames.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Soft** corner radiuses (`roundedness: 1`), balancing strict industrial architecture with bespoke tailoring softness.

### Corner Rules
- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Badges):** `0.25rem` (4px) border radius for crisp edges.
- **Container Elements (Cards, Panels):** `0.5rem` (8px) (`rounded-lg`) radius to frame garment imagery without distorting edge seams.
- **Structural Dividers:** Crisp `1px` solid borders without rounding, maintaining clean vertical and horizontal seams across data grids.

## Components

### Buttons & Conversion Triggers
- **Primary RFQ Button:** Dark Navy (`#0F172A`) solid background, crisp `#FFFFFF` uppercase text, `0.25rem` radius, padding `0.75rem 1.5rem`. Always paired with the gold trailing arrow: `ÜRETİM TEKLİFİ AL ↗`. On hover, background shifts to `#1E293B` with a subtle gold bottom stroke (`2px solid #C5A059`).
- **Secondary Action Button:** Transparent background, `1px solid #CBD5E1`, dark slate text. Hover shifts to `#FFFFFF` surface with `#C5A059` border outline.
- **Download Action:** Outlined or soft tint `#F8FAFC` button with persistent `↗` glyph for technical catalog and spec-sheet exports.

### Category & Collection Cards
- Structured 4-column or 2-column modules framed with `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- **Card Header:** Eyebrow index with gold accent (`01 / KOLEKSİYON`), followed by a serif headline (`Deniz Şortu`), summary description, and an anchored drill-down trigger (`İNCELE ↗`).
- **Image Container:** Strict aspect ratios (3:4 portrait or 16:10 landscape) with subtle zoom transition (`scale 1.03` over 400ms ease) on parent hover.

### Language Switcher
- Compact horizontal inline list: `TR | EN | AR | RU | FA`.
- Pipe dividers (`|`) styled in `#CBD5E1`.
- Inactive items set in `#64748B` with hover transition to `#0F172A`.
- Active locale set in `#0F172A` with a bold weight and subtle `#C5A059` underline indicator (`2px` offset). Supports RTL layout adjustments automatically when `AR` or `FA` is engaged.

### Production Metric Badges
- Structural pills displaying factory output indicators (e.g., `MIN. MOQ: 500 ADET`, `AYLIK KAPASİTE: 120.000 PCS`).
- Background: `#F8FAFC`, border: `1px solid #E2E8F0`, typography: `11px` bold `Plus Jakarta Sans`, text color: `#334155`. Left-hand accent indicator rendered as a `6px` solid `#C5A059` circle.

### Input Fields (RFQ Form System)
- Clean, structured form fields with floating or pinned uppercase label (`11px`, `#64748B`).
- Input container: `#FFFFFF` fill, `1px solid #CBD5E1` border, `0.25rem` radius, height `48px`, padding `0 1rem`. Focus state triggers `1px solid #C5A059` and a muted `0 0 0 3px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.15)` focus ring.
